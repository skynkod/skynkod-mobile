## 4. What Skynkod does

Skynkod helps you take care of your skin:

- reads a product's ingredient list (INCI) and shows how well it suits the skin profile you entered;
- creates and tracks morning and evening routines;
- keeps a daily skin journal and spots trends in your own entries;
- offers 7-day plans ("Emergency mode");
- tracks your products, when you opened them, and your spending (Skynbudget);
- provides educational articles (Skyncyclopedia);
- includes Koda, a chat assistant powered by artificial intelligence.
